THE CHECKUP

What I actually check.

Entra ID
This is your Microsoft 365 sign-in system. I check who has MFA and who doesn't, how many people have full admin rights, whether old sign-in methods that skip MFA are still turned on, and which outside apps and guest accounts can get into your email and files.
Active Directory
If you have a server in the office, this is what runs your logins. I look for accounts belonging to people who left years ago, service accounts that haven't had a password change since they were set up, and admin groups nobody has looked at in a long time.
Azure
If you run anything in Microsoft's cloud, I check what's open to the internet that shouldn't be, who has permission to change things, whether your backups would actually restore, and what you're paying for that nobody is using.
I also look for money.
Unused licenses, and cloud resources somebody forgot to turn off. I've found them in almost every environment I've cleaned up over fourteen years. It's pretty common for the savings to cover the checkup on their own.
